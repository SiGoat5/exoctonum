EIGHT MEN. ONE BOND.

Eight separate paths came together in 2013.

The eight spokes represent the men. The hub marks the point at which their paths joined. The unbroken circle represents the bond that surrounds them.

Different lives. Different journeys. One friendship carried forward.

I · II · III · IV · V · VI · VII · VIII
I

LUKE

Greek & Latin tradition · associated with light

Thoughtfulness and learning have long accompanied his name.

Observes carefully before reaching judgement.

Has compassion for those who suffer.

Seeks understanding rather than easy answers.

His name recalls Luke the physician, healer and chronicler.

Values truth and takes care in its telling.

Brings light through knowledge, compassion and understanding.

His tradition is one of healing, learning and illuminating what is true.

II

JEFF

Germanic origin · peaceful pledge

Peace is a strength he carries within.

Places great value on promises made and faithfully kept.

Seeks harmony where others might find conflict.

Steadfastness is part of his nature.

Believes that strength need not be loud to endure.

Faith and constancy guide the path he follows.

Knows the worth of trust, once given.

Finds strength in bringing people together.

Carries with him an ancient association with peace, faithfulness and keeping one's word.

III

DAVID

Hebrew origin · beloved

Courage has followed his name since ancient times.

Does not measure strength by size or appearance.

Faces what seems greater than himself with resolve.

His name recalls the shepherd who became a king.

Leadership is strengthened by loyalty and tempered by experience.

Music, poetry and faith belong to his ancient tradition.

Knows that even the powerful must remain capable of humility.

His name carries the enduring qualities of courage, devotion, leadership and the beloved.

IV

CRAIG

Scottish Gaelic · creag · rock or crag

Strength and solidity stand at the foundation of his name.

Remains firm when circumstances change around him.

Reliability is valued more highly than display.

Offers others something steady upon which to depend.

Has the endurance of the rock from which his name comes.

Does not easily yield when his course is certain.

Knows that true strength is often quiet and immovable.

His name carries the ancient qualities of steadfastness, endurance, strength and dependability.

V

SIMON

Hebrew origin · he who hears

Patient is how others describe him.

Is fortified by a strong faith in himself.

Has a thoughtful and questioning mind.

Guided by the sure and the true.

Knows that life is made more beautiful by sharing.

On his journey he appreciates each and every step.

Initiates many good plans.

VI

SEÁN

Irish form of John · God is gracious

Grace lies at the heart of his name.

Values what is given rather than merely possessed.

Has strength grounded in humility.

Faith and conviction have followed his name through generations.

Knows that generosity is greatest when freely offered.

Meets good fortune with gratitude.

Carries one of Ireland's most enduring names.

His name speaks of grace, faith, gratitude and generosity of spirit.

VII

SCOTT

Ancient name · a Scot · one from Scotland

Carries in his name a strong sense of place and belonging.

Independence has long been part of its inheritance.

Values loyalty to his people and to his roots.

Meets hardship with endurance.

Has a practical strength that does not need display.

Tradition is remembered, but does not prevent him forging his own path.

Stands firm when circumstances demand it.

His name carries associations of heritage, independence, loyalty and resilience.

VIII

CONALL

Ancient Irish & Gaelic · strong wolf

Courage has followed his name through the ages.

Loyalty is given fiercely and held steadfastly.

Strength is used to protect what he holds dear.

Has the spirit of the warrior, but the devotion of the guardian.

Leadership comes through courage and resolve.

Does not easily turn from a path once chosen.

Carries the ancient qualities of the wolf — strength, loyalty and fearlessness.

Like Conall Cernach, “Conall the Victorious,” meets adversity with an unconquered spirit.

His name has endured through centuries of Irish history, associated with warriors, heroes and kings.
